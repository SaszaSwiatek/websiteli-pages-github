Profile

Space for fresh perspectives and concrete next steps.

KreativChaos by Mirjana Jaksic Jelic brings together consulting, coaching and training at the intersection of information and knowledge management, artificial intelligence, digital communication and collaboration.

The focus is on learning and innovation processes that can help people, teams and organisations explore new possibilities together.
