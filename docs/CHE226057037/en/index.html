Our focus

One clear purpose. Three perspectives.

The association has a non-commercial purpose: it supports financial products intended to advance innovation and the functioning of international financial markets while broadening access to Swiss real estate.

01 / ACCESS

Broadening opportunities

Financial products are intended to make Swiss real estate accessible to the broadest possible group of investors.

02 / ISSUANCE

Supporting financial products

Pro Themelio 1 supports the issuance of financial instruments and investments within its association purpose.

03 / NETWORK

Connecting expertise

The association may engage qualified external service providers for specific activities.

Our approach
