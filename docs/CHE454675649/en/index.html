Our focus

Thinking real estate. Advancing projects.

Novura2 AG operates in and around Zurich across real estate, building land and construction projects. Its focus is on combining real estate expertise with development and delivery.

This creates a field of activity that covers existing real estate and land as well as the development and execution of construction projects.
