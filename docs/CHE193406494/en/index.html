What matters

Challenging situations do not have to have the final say.

ZUENDHOLZ is for employed people and people in ongoing education who are dealing with psychosocial strain. The focus is on inner direction and a practical ability to act.

Focus areas

Four strengths that make movement possible.

01

Orientation

Bring structure to the situation, recognise priorities and find a fitting direction.

02

Self-care

Notice personal limits and needs, then consciously account for them in daily life.

03

Self-efficacy

Recognise personal possibilities and strengthen confidence in one’s own actions.

04

Ability to act

Turn clarity into concrete and sustainable next steps.

Professional background

Understanding people and organisations.

Michele Frei combines a master’s degree in social, organisational and business psychology with extensive experience in human resources, project work and transformation.

This perspective supports an understanding of personal situations as well as the work and education environments in which they arise.
