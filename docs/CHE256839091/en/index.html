Profile

Curator, cultural anthropologist, archivist.

Michael Hiltbrunner works as an independent curator and is based in Zurich. His background connects art theory, cultural anthropology, and archival work.

His practice includes curatorial projects, opening up art archives, and developing publications and web platforms in the field of contemporary art.
