Profile

A long-term view of real estate.

The Myrrhen AG works with real estate and real-estate projects. Its focus is on considering properties and plans carefully through their different stages of development.

The approach is deliberately clear: rather than putting individual projects in the foreground, it brings together development, management and long-term questions of value.

Approach

Understanding substance. Thinking ahead.

Real estate calls for decisions that reach beyond the present moment. The Myrrhen AG therefore combines an eye for development with attention to what carries value over time.
